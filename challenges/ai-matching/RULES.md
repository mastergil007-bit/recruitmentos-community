# Challenge Rules

- Use only synthetic challenge data. Never upload real CVs or personally identifiable information.
- Return structured, explainable results grounded in candidate and job evidence. Do not rely solely on exact keyword matching.
- Document the reasoning behind your approach and its known limitations. Any AI model, framework or architecture may be used.
- Clearly document external APIs and services, including what synthetic data they receive.
- Human review remains part of recruitment decisions. This challenge is decision support, not autonomous hiring.
- Do not use or infer protected characteristics. Evaluate candidates only on job-relevant information.
- Do not expose credentials, API keys or secrets in submissions.
- Do not include client data or private Recruitment OS algorithms or implementation details.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for submission guidance.
